Three days in Copenhagen is the perfect amount of time to explore the Danish capital. The city is ideal for exploring on foot or—like the locals—by bicycle. This varied and logistically optimized 3-day itinerary combines key highlights with a relaxed Scandinavian lifestyle.

Discovering Copenhagen’s canals is one of the most delightful experiences in the Danish capital. From the water, you can admire the shimmering canals, historic colorful gabled houses, and modern Scandinavian design from a whole new perspective.

The three best-known and most important sights in Copenhagen, which should not be missed on any visit, are:

  • Nyhavn (The New Harbor) The picturesque harbor canal from the 17th century is the absolute symbol of the city.
  • Tivoli Gardens (Tivoli)Opened in 1843, Tivoli is one of the oldest amusement parks in the world and is located in the heart of the city, directly opposite the central station.
  • Amalienborg Palace is the winter residence of the Danish royal family; it consists of four identical palaces lining a magnificent, octagonal palace square.

Copenhagen, the Danish capital, is one of the most livable and bicycle-friendly cities in the world. The city harmoniously blends historic architecture and royal history with modern Scandinavian design and innovative urban planning. Its most famous landmarks include the colorful Nyhavn harbor, the world-renowned Tivoli Gardens amusement park, and the iconic Little Mermaid statue.

Pro tipps
  1. Explore the city like the locals do—by bike: Copenhagen is the ultimate cycling city. The infrastructure is perfectly developed, and you can often get around faster on two wheels than by metro. You can easily rent bikes on practically every corner using. various apps
  2. Take to the water for free or low-cost sightseeing: instead of expensive tours, you can use the yellow harbor buses (*Havnebussen*)—they are part of the public transport network, so you can cruise through the harbor for the price of a standard ticket.
